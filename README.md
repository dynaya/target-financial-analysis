# Target Corporation Financial Analysis

**Part 1: Ratio Analysis | Fiscal 2020–2024 | MBA 440 | Group 6 | Illinois State University | Fall 2026**

This group project examines Target Corporation’s financial performance using income statement and balance sheet data. Part 1 covers the company overview, liquidity, efficiency, debt, profitability, DuPont analysis, and market ratios. The repository includes our report, Excel calculations, class presentation, and the instructor’s project brief.

## My contribution

I am Debbie Ampong, a member of Group 6. I was responsible for the **General Overview** and **Efficiency Analysis** sections, and I presented both sections to our class and instructor.

For the general overview, I summarized Target’s business and compared sales, operating income, net earnings, and total assets across the five years. ## Findings from my sections

### General overview

Financial amounts below are in **$ millions** and come from the workbook’s `Financial Data` sheet.

| Measure | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Sales | 92,400 | 104,611 | 107,588 | 105,803 | 106,566 |
| Operating income | 6,539 | 8,946 | 3,848 | 5,707 | 5,566 |
| Net earnings | 4,368 | 6,946 | 2,780 | 4,138 | 4,091 |
| Total assets | 51,248 | 53,811 | 53,335 | 55,356 | 57,769 |

Sales and total assets were higher in 2024 than in 2020, while 2024 net earnings remained below the 2020 level. Operating income fell sharply in 2022 and then partially recovered.

### Efficiency analysis

The results below reproduce the workbook’s `Efficiency` sheet, rounded to two decimal places. Turnover ratios are expressed in times; timing measures are expressed in days.

| Measure | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Total asset turnover | 1.97 | 1.99 | 2.01 | 1.95 | 1.88 |
| Inventory turnover | 6.74 | 6.11 | 6.00 | 6.12 | 6.21 |
| Days inventory outstanding | 54.18 | 59.78 | 60.81 | 59.60 | 58.75 |
| Accounts receivable turnover* | 63.18 | 62.42 | 55.49 | 53.91 | 56.70 |
| Average collection period* | 5.78 | 5.85 | 6.58 | 6.77 | 6.44 |
| Accounts payable turnover | 5.81 | 5.29 | 5.68 | 6.08 | 6.08 |
| Average payment period | 62.82 | 68.99 | 64.29 | 60.07 | 60.00 |
| Operating cycle* | 59.95 | 65.63 | 67.39 | 66.37 | 65.18 |
| Cash conversion cycle* | -2.87 | -3.36 | 3.11 | 6.30 | 5.19 |

*The workbook uses other current assets as a proxy for receivables. Collection and cycle measures therefore depend on that assumption; see the [methodology notes](docs/methodology.md).

Target generated less sales revenue per dollar of average assets in 2024 than in 2020. Inventory movement improved after 2022, but inventory still stayed on hand longer than in 2020. The calculated cash conversion cycle changed from -2.87 days in 2020 to 5.19 days in 2024, indicating a longer interval between supplier payments and cash collection under the workbook’s assumptions.

My recommendations focused on improving demand forecasting and replenishment to reduce inventory time, and reviewing supplier payment timing in relation to the inventory cycle.
